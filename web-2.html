The quiet cost of a bad website

Your website might be sending customers to your competitors.

Most visitors decide in seconds whether to stay. When the site lets them down, they don't complain — they just leave. Here's what that usually looks like:

01 — Problem

It loads slowly

Impact: people hit "back" before the page appears — and the ad click you paid for is wasted.

02 — Problem

Nobody can tell what you do

Impact: a vague headline and generic stock photos make visitors work too hard, so they go back to search.

03 — Problem

It breaks on phones

Impact: tiny buttons, sideways scrolling and pinch-to-zoom push away the majority of your visitors.

04 — Problem

There's no clear next step

Impact: the phone number is buried and the form is long — interested buyers drift off without contacting you.

05 — Problem

Search engines can't read it

Impact: messy structure and missing local signals mean competitors take the clicks you should be getting.

06 — Problem

Enquiries die in an inbox

Impact: form emails sit unread over the weekend, and by Monday that lead has hired someone else.

How will you get the leads?

3. Make contact easy

Mobile-first websites

Designed for a thumb first, a desktop second.

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone — often in a hurry. So we design the small screen first and scale up, instead of squeezing a desktop layout down.

  • Sticky call & quote bar that follows the visitor down the page
  • Large tap targets — no zooming in to hit a link
  • Compressed, lazy-loaded images for quick loads on mobile data
  • Short, smart forms that open the right keyboard for each field
  • Readable type at every size, without pinch-to-zoom
  • Core Web Vitals checked before launch, not after
